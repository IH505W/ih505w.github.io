Privacy policy

This policy describes Google data access and processing by n8n Personal Automation, a private project operated by its owner, IH505W. Features are configured and tested in stages, and access depends on connections explicitly authorized by the owner.

Google data accessed

The owner explicitly authorizes connections through Google OAuth. The inbox-reading connection requests gmail.readonly access to messages and settings. Workflows may retrieve message identifiers, thread identifiers, headers, senders, recipients, subjects, snippets, and message bodies. Full-message responses may include attachment content within the original email; attachment-specific processing is not part of the current workflow.

The Calendar connection requests calendar.freebusy access. It retrieves busy time intervals for calendars and time windows selected in a workflow. It does not request permission to read event titles or descriptions, or to create, change, or delete events.

A separate optional draft-writing connection requests gmail.compose. When authorized, this enables storing suggested reply bodies, recipients, subjects, and reply-thread information as drafts in Gmail, and accessing draft information when needed to manage the workflow. Google bundles sending email and managing drafts, including draft deletion, into this permission. The automated workflow is configured to create drafts for owner review and does not automatically send email or delete drafts.

OAuth credentials and tokens maintain the authorized connections. They are used by fixed n8n operations and are not provided to the AI model. Future features requiring broader access will require an updated policy and Google consent.

How data is used

Email content is used for local summarization, categorization, assessment of reply needs, and preparation of suggested reply text. Selected reply text and original message details may be saved to Gmail as a draft through the separately authorized draft-writing connection. Availability information is used for scheduling assistance. The owner reviews, edits, and sends replies manually.

Local workflow records may track message identifiers, processing status, assessments, and draft identifiers to support troubleshooting and avoid repeated processing. Owner notifications are a planned capability; additional notification destinations and any associated sharing will be documented before being enabled.

Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train a general-purpose AI model. Google data will be used only to provide the functionality described in this policy. Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Processing, storage, and sharing

Retrieved Google data is processed in the owner's private n8n installation. AI analysis and reply preparation run on the owner's locally hosted Ollama server. Message data, assessments, and suggested replies may be retained in local workflow execution records depending on n8n's execution-saving settings. Credentials, tokens, and workflow tracking records are stored by the private n8n installation.

Google hosts the source mailbox and calendars and processes API requests according to Google's own policies. Saved drafts are stored in the owner's authorized Gmail account. Email content, calendar data, and OAuth credentials are not uploaded to this public website or its GitHub repository. If notifications or future integrations would share Google data with an additional service, the owner will document that service and sharing here before enabling it.

Retention and deletion

Local execution records, tracking records, and other workflow-generated data remain until deleted by the owner or removed by configured retention rules. This setup has not established a fixed retention period. Copies included in backups may remain until those backups are removed or expire. Saved Gmail drafts remain in Gmail until the owner edits, sends, or deletes them.

The owner can stop workflows, delete stored execution records and generated files, and remove credentials from n8n. Google authorization can be revoked through the account's third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete data already stored locally or drafts already saved in Gmail. Deleting local copies does not delete the original Gmail messages or Calendar events.

Public website

This informational site uses no application analytics, tracking scripts, or email submission forms. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before data access or processing practices change. The support email displayed on the Google consent screen is the contact address for this private integration.
