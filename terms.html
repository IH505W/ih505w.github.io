Terms of use

n8n Personal Automation is a private integration for its owner. This website provides information about the project.

Intended use

The project provides personal email review, local AI analysis and reply preparation, and calendar availability checks using accounts the owner is authorized to access. Inbox reading uses a read-only Gmail connection, and Calendar checks use free/busy access. An optional, separately authorized Gmail compose connection saves drafts. Features are configured and tested in stages, and availability depends on the workflows the owner enables.

There is no public signup, paid subscription, or offer of hosted automation to other users. Use of a university-managed Google account remains subject to that institution's rules and access controls.

Owner review

The owner remains responsible for reviewing suggested replies and scheduling information. Replies are prepared for the owner to review, edit, and send manually in Gmail. Google's compose permission also allows sending email and managing drafts, but the automated workflow does not send email or delete drafts. AI output may be inaccurate and should be checked before use.

Availability

This personal integration has no guaranteed uptime or support commitment. Google access depends on account authorization, API availability, network connectivity, and any applicable administrator policies. Google, n8n, Ollama, and GitHub are separate products governed by their own applicable terms.

Stopping access

The owner can disable workflows and revoke Google authorization at any time. Local records and backups may need to be deleted separately, as described in the privacy policy.

Contact

The support email displayed on the Google consent screen is the contact address for the integration. Project ownership is identified publicly as IH505W.
